# 📱 War Mode: Native Android Standalone App

A pure obsidian black single-button digital bodyguard app.
Zero APIs. Zero external servers. Zero background battery drain.

---

## 🚀 How to Open & Run in Android Studio

1. Open **Android Studio**.
2. Select **Open** and choose this directory:
   `C:\Users\Sahithi\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\war-mode\android-app`
3. Let Gradle sync for 15-30 seconds.
4. Plug in your Android phone via USB (with **USB Debugging** enabled in Developer Options) or start an Android Emulator.
5. Click the green **Run (Play)** button `▶` at the top of Android Studio.

---

## ⚙️ Essential Phone Settings (First Launch)

When you open the app on your phone:
1. **Allow Notifications** when prompted.
2. Tap the bottom hint: **"Set battery usage to Unrestricted"** (allows the hardware alarm to wake up your phone while deep in your pocket).
3. Tap **⚡ TEST ALERT NOW** to verify the double-strike vibration pattern and heads-up banner on your lock screen.
4. Tap **ENGAGE WAR MODE** to arm the 15-minute timer.
